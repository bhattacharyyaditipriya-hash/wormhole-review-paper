# Traversing the Unknown
### Wormholes, Time, and the Architecture of the Multiverse

This repository contains the review paper **"Traversing the Unknown: Wormholes, Time, and the Architecture of the Multiverse."**

The paper explores the theoretical foundations of traversable wormholes, general relativity, quantum mechanics, spacetime geometry, time travel, and multiverse models. Rather than proposing a new theory, it synthesizes decades of scientific literature into a single accessible review aimed at students, educators, and science enthusiasts.

---

## Abstract

Wormholes have long occupied the boundary between theoretical physics and science fiction. Although predicted by Einstein's field equations, their existence remains unconfirmed and depends upon physical conditions that have yet to be observed. This review examines the current theoretical understanding of wormholes, including their mathematical foundations, stability, energy requirements, potential relationship to time travel, and their role within various multiverse hypotheses. By combining research from general relativity, quantum field theory, and modern cosmology, this paper presents a broad overview of one of the most fascinating concepts in modern physics while highlighting the unanswered questions that continue to drive scientific investigation.
